The viewer at /

The top card sits where the walking HUD will put it in tawaf. While the scene loads, the card says so on a plain backdrop; when the first frame is drawn, the backdrop fades out over 320ms and the card shows the instruction.

When the 3D view can't be shown

Two cases, one layout. With no WebGL 2, the page stays on the plain backdrop and says what to do. If the browser drops the 3D view while it is open, the backdrop comes back over the frozen scene and offers a reload.

The stress page at /perf

A developer page; nothing links to it. It draws a fixed load round the Kaabah, 100 columns of about 2,000 triangles each and one walking figure, with culling off so the load never changes with the camera angle. The counts below are read from the renderer, not typed in. The running frame is live: its FPS is this preview page's, not a phone's. The pass and below-30 frames show example readings.
