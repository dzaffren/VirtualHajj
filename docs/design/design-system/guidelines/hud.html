Walking HUD

  1. Put text only on an opaque surface or sunken panel. The scene is never a text background.
  2. Keep the lower middle clear while the character walks. Put controls in the bottom corners only.
  3. Show one instruction at a time, in the top card.

  5. Never show a score, a timer, a streak or a leaderboard.
